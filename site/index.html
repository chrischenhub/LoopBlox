02 / The research process

The evidence chooses what stays.

One frozen benchmark. Reusable Python Loops. A new research conversation after each successful checkpoint.

  1. 01

    Propose

    Inspect evidence. Save a candidate and its hypothesis.

  2. 02

    Evaluate

    Run the same ten tasks with fresh environments and official scoring.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Complete Jev analysis and inspect behavior, outcomes and cost.

  4. 04

    Keep the best

    The host ranks passes, then agent input tokens, then model attempts.

  5. 05 ↺

    Checkpoint

    Preserve evidence and notes. Start the next research iteration.

Research continues until stopped. Final test evaluation requires separate review and authorization.
