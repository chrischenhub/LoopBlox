# Retired experiment workflows

Archived September 21, 2026 when research moved to continuous Loop improvement
on one frozen benchmark. `manifest.json` records the exact bytes copied from the
mutable checkout before cleanup, including uncommitted work.

The archive contains random screening, BFS/DFS, specialist/mixed-domain studies,
inheritance conditions, retail rounds, the bounded release and their shared
helpers. It also preserves the former benchmark CLI, suite preparation, research
session and τ² condition before removing their obsolete branches.

These files are source records, not a self-contained runnable package or supported
entry points. Reproduce a historical campaign with that campaign's complete frozen
`implementation/`, protocol and dependencies. No campaign artifacts, recorded
outcomes, costs or earlier archives were changed by this cleanup.
